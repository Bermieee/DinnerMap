Dinner calendar

Takeout days contain no recipe ingredients and are excluded from grocery lists. Curated cuisine collections include published 1× ingredient measurements and link to complete cooking instructions on their respective recipe sites; these selections remain browsable offline. Randomizing a week/month keeps takeout days in place; choosing a recipe on a takeout day replaces it. Additional searchable recipes and ingredient measures are supplied by TheMealDB, a community recipe database. Quantities are for each recipe's original yield, not automatically scaled for a family of four. Shopping totals only combine compatible, quantified measurements; different package sizes and unmeasured amounts remain separate. Extra sides are not included unless the linked recipe lists them. An internet connection is needed the first time a recipe loads; previously loaded recipes are cached in this browser.
